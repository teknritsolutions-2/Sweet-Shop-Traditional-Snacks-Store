MANY REGIONS. MANY TRADITIONS.

The beauty is
in the details.

Across India, sweet-making takes many forms: milk reduced into rich solids, flour roasted for depth, nuts ground into fine pastes, or cooked sweets finished in syrup.

Each technique creates its own character. The fine bite of kaju katli and the layered crumble of soan papdi tell very different stories.

These are general traditions, rather than a description of a particular store’s recipe or production process.
