BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few helpful details.

Can I place a bulk order?+

Yes. Build an enquiry with your occasion, quantity and preferred date on our Bulk & Festival Orders page.

How do I choose a gift assortment?+

Explore collections by occasion and budget, then include your preferred selection in an enquiry. Contents and packaging are confirmed before an order is accepted.

Where can I find allergen details?+

Please request verified ingredient and allergen information before ordering. Product formulations can vary.

How much lead time should I allow?+

Include your required date in the enquiry. Lead time, delivery arrangements and availability must be confirmed before an order is accepted.
