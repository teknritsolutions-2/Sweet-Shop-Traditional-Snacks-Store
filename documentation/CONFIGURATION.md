# Store configuration

Before accepting customer orders, replace indicative collection prices and pack sizes with approved product records; supply verified ingredient, allergen and storage information. Confirm available assortments and packaging. The catalog centralizes this data in products.js.

Store address, map, contact channels and business hours are deliberately not invented. Connect verified contact details and an enquiry backend if delivery of requests is desired. Current forms retain data only in the page and provide a downloadable request.

Theme, direction and cart are stored independently in the visitor's browser. Form input is never interpreted as HTML. Inputs enforce browser constraints, quantity bounds and a local-date minimum.

Image credits are in photo-sources.json and the linked public credits page. Preserve the attribution and applicable share-alike image license when reusing assets.

Contact is the only enquiry form. Open `pages/contact.html?enquiry=bulk` for celebration fields; optional `product` (catalog ID), `occasion`, `quantity` and `packaging` parameters prefill editable fields. `?cart=1` retains the selected bag items in General mode. The full Gift Explorer lives on Bulk & Festival Orders and creates the corresponding Contact URL.

The client clarified that a new original name was desired. The storefront identity is Mithmora, paired with the existing sweet-diamond symbol. Header and footer share the section content width; navigation centers independently of the logo and utility widths. Product cards use explicit minus/plus quantity steppers with direct numeric entry and bounds of 1–10,000.
